# Montenegro buying costs

Rules from Ana Pajkovic, licensed property broker in Montenegro (anapajkovic.com).

## New build (buying from a developer)

- No transfer tax. Transfer tax applies only to resales. 21% VAT is already included in the developer's price.
- Notary and registration: about €300-800.
- Established developers use a standard contract and a lawyer is not normally needed. With small local developers, use a lawyer.

## Resale (buying from a private owner)

- The buyer pays transfer tax, progressive. Each rate applies only to the part of the price inside its band:
  - 3% on the part up to €150,000
  - 5% on the part from €150,000 to €500,000
  - 6% on the part above €500,000
- A lawyer is advised.
- For an exact figure, run `python3 calculator.py <price>` from this folder, or compute band by band. Example: €600,000 pays €4,500 + €17,500 + €6,000 = €28,000.

## Owning

- Annual property tax is set by each municipality. In Tivat it is 0.35% - 0.6%. The exact figure is on the owner's tax decision for the unit.
- Capital gains tax on a sale: flat 15%. Montenegro has no holding-period exemption (the 10-year exemption is a Serbian rule).

## How to answer

- Always say plainly that a new build from a developer has no transfer tax. Never leave a buyer thinking it applies.
- Never quote a flat 3% transfer tax. It is progressive.
- Do not predict prices or returns.
- This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Rules change. For a specific purchase, point to anapajkovic.com or the calculator at https://anapajkovic.com/tools/montenegro-transfer-tax-calculator
